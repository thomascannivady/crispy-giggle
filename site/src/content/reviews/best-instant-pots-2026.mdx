An Instant Pot is really three decisions in one: how many functions do you actually need, how
much capacity, and how much counter space are you willing to give up. We compared a
full-featured model, the classic all-rounder, and a compact option built for smaller households.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced multiple independent multi-cooker comparisons and lab-style test results,
focusing on genuine functional differences (not just marketing feature counts), capacity, and
each model's track record and review history as a reliability signal. We did not receive any of
these products for free from the manufacturers, and we didn't test physical units ourselves for
this post — the pros and cons above reflect what turned up consistently across independent
sources, not firsthand testing.
