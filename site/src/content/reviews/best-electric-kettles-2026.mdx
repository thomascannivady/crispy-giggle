If you've started making pour-over coffee at home, a standard kettle makes it hard to control
the pour rate — which is most of what separates a good cup from a great one. We compared three
gooseneck electric kettles that are consistently well-reviewed for home use.

## How we compared these

We looked at pour control (spout shape and flow consistency), how precisely each kettle holds a
target temperature, heat-up time, and price relative to features. We did not receive any of these
products for free from the manufacturers.
