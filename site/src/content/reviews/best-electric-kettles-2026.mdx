If you've started making pour-over coffee at home, a standard kettle makes it hard to control
the pour rate — which is most of what separates a good cup from a great one. We compared three
gooseneck electric kettles that consistently show up as top picks across independent coffee-gear
reviews, rather than picking whatever ranks highest in a plain Amazon search.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced multiple independent gooseneck-kettle comparisons and coffee-gear review
sites, then checked pour control (spout shape and flow rate), temperature precision, capacity,
and price against what those reviews reported — not just marketing copy. We did not receive any
of these products for free from the manufacturers, and we didn't test physical units ourselves
for this post; the pros and cons above reflect what turned up consistently across independent
sources, not firsthand testing.

None of these are the cheapest gooseneck kettles you can find — they're the ones that kept
showing up as the best-differentiated options: one built for precision, one for value, one for
capacity.
