These solve slightly different problems: one brews a full carafe, one gives you pod-or-grounds
flexibility, and one is the simplest, cheapest, most-proven option if all you want is a
push-button iced coffee.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced real amazon.ca review data (rating, review volume, and current pricing) for
all three, along with each product's documented brewing capabilities (single-serve vs. carafe,
pod vs. grounds compatibility). We did not receive any of these products for free from the
manufacturers, and we didn't test physical units ourselves for this post — the pros and cons
above reflect verified review data and product specifications, not firsthand testing.
