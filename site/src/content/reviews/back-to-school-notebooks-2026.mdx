A notebook that needs to survive a full school year of being stuffed in a backpack is a
different product than one that just needs to hold notes for a semester.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced Five Star's specific durability claims (ink-bleed resistance, water-resistant
cover) against independent owner reviews describing real long-term use, and looked for
comparable independent claims about the Amazon Basics equivalent. We did not receive either
product for free, and we didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect
independent sourcing, not firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

We couldn't find strong independent evidence that Amazon Basics matches Five Star's specific,
testable durability claims (ink-bleed resistance, water-resistant cover, reinforced binding) —
those are genuine, sourced advantages for Five Star, not just brand reputation. Amazon Basics is
the cheaper option, and that's a legitimate reason to choose it if the notebook just needs to
get through a semester. For something that needs to survive a full year of daily use, Five
Star has the better-documented case.
