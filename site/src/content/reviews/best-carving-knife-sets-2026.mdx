If you carve a turkey or ham once or twice a year, the real question is whether "buy once for
decades" is worth roughly double the price of a knife that's still genuinely well-regarded on
its own.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced independent carving knife comparisons and long-term owner reviews,
specifically looking for documented edge-retention claims over years of use, not just
out-of-the-box sharpness. We did not receive either product for free, and we didn't test
physical units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect independent sourcing, not firsthand
testing.
