Both pitchers are independently certified for lead reduction — the real difference is filter
cost predictability versus maximum filtration performance.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced independent water filter pitcher testing, focusing on documented
certifications (lead, PFOA/PFOS reduction) and real filter replacement costs over time rather
than just upfront price. We did not receive either product for free, and we didn't test physical
units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect independent sourcing, not firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

If your water isn't unusually high in dissolved solids, Brita Elite's predictable 6-month filter
life makes it the more practical value pick — you know exactly what it costs to run. ZeroWater
is the stronger choice if you specifically want to verify and minimize total dissolved solids,
but budget for filters that may need replacing more often depending on your water source.
