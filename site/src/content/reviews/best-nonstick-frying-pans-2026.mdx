Every non-stick pan eventually loses some of its release — the real differences are in how well
each one sears, how long the coating holds up, and what it's made of. We compared a budget PTFE
pan, a stainless-hybrid pan built for searing, and a ceramic pan built around avoiding PTFE
entirely.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced multiple independent cookware reviews and lab-style abrasion testing
(Consumer Reports, America's Test Kitchen, and dedicated cookware review sites), focusing on
release performance, coating durability under real wear, and heat retention. We did not receive
any of these products for free from the manufacturers, and we didn't test physical units
ourselves for this post — the pros and cons above reflect what turned up consistently across
independent sources, not firsthand testing.
