Index cards are about as close to a commodity product as office supplies get — which makes it
one of the more clear-cut comparisons in this batch.

## How we compared these

We referenced a direct independent thickness/quality comparison between the two heavyweight
card lines, plus verified Amazon review data for Oxford. We did not receive either product for
free, and we didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect independent
sourcing, not firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

This is a genuine, well-supported Amazon Basics win. An independent side-by-side thickness test
found the two brands "almost the same," with Amazon Basics actually measuring slightly thicker —
meaning you're not sacrificing quality for the lower price. Unless you specifically want Oxford's
brand name or its USA-made positioning, Amazon Basics is the better value here on the actual
evidence, not just the cheaper label.
