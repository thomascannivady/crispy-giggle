Clipboards are a rare category where independent reviewers have actually stress-tested several
brands side by side.

## How we compared these

We referenced an independent heavy-duty clipboard ranking that scored multiple brands
head-to-head, plus verified Amazon review data for both products. We did not receive either
product for free, and we didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect
independent sourcing, not firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

Both are genuinely well-reviewed. Amazon Basics is a real, solid product — one ranking even
called it the best-rated storage clipboard by review volume and score — but in a direct
heavy-duty comparison it placed behind both Saunders and Officemate. For typical school or light
office use, Amazon Basics is the practical value pick. If the clipboard needs to survive years
of genuinely heavy daily use, Saunders has the better-documented track record.
