A roasting pan is a once-or-twice-a-year purchase for most people, which makes the "how many
years will this actually last" question more relevant than it seems.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced multiple independent roasting pan tests and reviews, focusing on
construction quality, heat distribution, and what's actually included (lid, rack style) rather
than just capacity. We did not receive any of these products for free, and we didn't test
physical units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect independent sourcing, not firsthand
testing.
