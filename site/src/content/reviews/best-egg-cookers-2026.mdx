An egg cooker is a small, cheap appliance category where brand name doesn't necessarily track
with the strongest real-world evidence — worth checking before assuming the bigger name is the
better buy.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced real amazon.ca review data (rating, review volume, and current pricing) for
all three, along with each product's documented capacity and features. We did not receive any of
these products for free from the manufacturers, and we didn't test physical units ourselves for
this post — the pros and cons above reflect verified review data and product specifications, not
firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

The Elite Gourmet has a genuinely stronger evidence case than the better-known Dash — nearly
double the review volume, a higher rating, and a lower price. Dash's Deluxe model still makes
sense if you specifically need to cook a dozen eggs at once for a full household; for typical
day-to-day use, Elite Gourmet is the harder pick to argue against on the numbers alone.
