Stainless steel and glass solve different problems here — stainless is lighter and unbreakable,
glass goes in the microwave and lets you see what's happening inside.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced independent mixing bowl testing and reviews, focusing on genuine material
tradeoffs (microwave safety, durability, weight) rather than just capacity and price. We did not
receive either product for free, and we didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros and cons
reflect independent sourcing, not firsthand testing.
