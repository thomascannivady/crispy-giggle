This comparison comes down to a genuine question: are you buying a mixer, or a long-term
platform you'll add attachments to over the years? The honest answer changes which one is
actually the better value.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced independent stand mixer comparisons and reviews, focusing on documented
reliability history and what each brand's attachment/parts ecosystem actually offers beyond the
base mixer. We did not receive either product for free, and we didn't test physical units
ourselves — the pros and cons reflect independent sourcing, not firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

KitchenAid's reputation here isn't just brand recognition — the attachment ecosystem is real,
documented, and genuinely extends the mixer's usefulness for years after purchase, which is
exactly the kind of after-sales value we're specifically looking for in these picks. If you just
need something that mixes cookie dough occasionally, Hamilton Beach is a legitimately
well-reviewed way to spend a fraction of the price.
