Two 12-packs, near-identical review scores, and both genuinely well-liked — this is one of the
closer calls in our roundups.

## How we compared these

We looked at review volume and score as real-world quality signals, plus what independent
sources and owner reviews said about ink performance (smudging, dry time, tip precision). We
did not receive either product for free, and we didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros
and cons reflect what we found from independent sourcing and verified Amazon review data, not
firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

We couldn't find strong evidence that one meaningfully outperforms the other for typical
highlighting use — both sit around 4.6–4.7 stars with tens of thousands of ratings. Amazon
Basics is the practical value pick mainly because it's usually priced at or below Sharpie for a
comparable pack, not because we found it to be a better product. If you already trust Sharpie as
a brand, there's no real reason found here to switch.
