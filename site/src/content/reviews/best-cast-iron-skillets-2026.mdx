Cast iron skillets are mostly about size and seasoning method — the material itself performs
similarly across brands once properly cared for, so review volume and price-per-size matter more
than any single "best" pick.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced real amazon.ca review data (rating, review volume, and current pricing)
across all three sizes, along with each product's seasoning method and stated use cases. We did
not receive any of these products for free from the manufacturers, and we didn't test physical
units ourselves for this post — the pros and cons above reflect verified review data and product
specifications, not firsthand testing.
