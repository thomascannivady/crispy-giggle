A full cookware set is a bigger commitment than a single pan, so review volume matters more here
than in almost any other category — a handful of five-star reviews doesn't tell you much about
how a set holds up across years of daily use.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced real amazon.ca review data (rating, review volume, and current pricing)
across all three sets, along with documented construction details (ply count, oven-safe
temperature, induction compatibility). We did not receive any of these products for free from
the manufacturers, and we didn't test physical units ourselves for this post — the pros and cons
above reflect verified review data and product specifications, not firsthand testing.
