This isn't really a "which is better" comparison — it's manual versus electric, and the honest
answer depends on whether hands-free operation is worth a shorter expected lifespan.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced independent can opener testing and reviews, specifically looking at
documented longevity differences between manual and electric mechanisms, not just initial
performance. We did not receive either product for free, and we didn't test physical units
ourselves — the pros and cons reflect independent sourcing, not firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

If longevity and value are the priority, the OXO manual opener has the stronger case — real
evidence of 15-20+ year lifespans, a huge review base, and a much lower price. The Cuisinart
electric is worth it specifically if hands-free operation matters to you (arthritis, limited
grip strength, or just preference) — but go in knowing electric can openers don't typically
match a good manual opener's lifespan.
