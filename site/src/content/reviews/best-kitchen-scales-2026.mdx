Kitchen scales are mostly a solved problem at this point — the real question is whether the
OXO's pull-out display is worth several times the price of a scale with 194,000+ positive
reviews behind it.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced independent kitchen scale testing and reviews, weighing build quality and
design features against real-world review volume as a reliability signal. We did not receive
either product for free, and we didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect
independent sourcing, not firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

This is a genuine case where the budget option has real, substantial evidence behind it — not
just a lower price. The Etekcity's review volume alone (194K+) makes it one of the most
battle-tested kitchen scales available, and independent sources specifically call it out as the
best-value pick in the category. The OXO's pull-out display is a real, useful feature if you
often weigh things in large bowls — but it's a convenience upgrade, not evidence of better core
accuracy or durability.
