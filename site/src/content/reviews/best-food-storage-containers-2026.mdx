Glass and plastic solve different problems here — glass lasts longer and handles heat better,
plastic is lighter, cheaper, and less likely to shatter in a bag.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced independent glass-vs-plastic testing and buying guides, focusing on
documented longevity, leakproof performance, and any manufacturer warranty backing the product —
not just price and piece count. We did not receive any of these products for free, and we
didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect independent sourcing, not
firsthand testing.
