This is a case where the premium option's higher price is backed by real, multi-decade
durability evidence — but the budget alternative's performance-per-dollar case is just as real.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced independent silicone baking mat testing and long-term owner reports,
specifically looking for documented lifespan claims rather than just initial performance. We did
not receive either product for free, and we didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros and
cons reflect independent sourcing, not firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

If you bake often enough that a mat lasting 15-20 years actually matters to your math, Silpat's
premium is genuinely justified by real evidence, not just brand reputation. If you bake
occasionally or want to try a few sizes without committing, the Amazon Basics set delivers real,
documented performance close to Silpat for a fraction of the price — a legitimate value pick,
not just "cheap."
