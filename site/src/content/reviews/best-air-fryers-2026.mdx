Air fryers range from single-serving countertop units to XL dual-basket machines that can replace
a second oven — the right one depends entirely on how many people you're cooking for and how much
counter space you're willing to give up. We compared three well-reviewed models that each solve a
different version of that problem.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced multiple independent air fryer reviews and test results, focusing on
capacity, cooking consistency, noise, and real owner-reported reliability rather than marketing
copy. One product we initially considered (the Ninja DZ401 DualZone XL) turned out to be listed
as unavailable on amazon.ca with no restock date, so we dropped it in favor of a comparable,
similarly well-reviewed in-stock model. We did not receive any of these products for free from
the manufacturers, and we didn't test physical units ourselves for this post — the pros and cons
above reflect what turned up consistently across independent sources, not firsthand testing.
