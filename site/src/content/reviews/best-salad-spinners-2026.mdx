## How we compared these

We cross-referenced independent salad spinner testing that specifically evaluated drying
performance and mechanism type (pump vs. crank vs. pull-cord), plus verified real Amazon review
data and current amazon.ca pricing for both. We did not receive either product for free, and we
didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect independent sourcing, not
firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

Normally we'd expect the well-known brand to have an edge somewhere, but the evidence here just
doesn't support paying more for the OXO. Farberware uses the same pump mechanism that testers
specifically prefer, and its own review numbers on amazon.ca are stronger. Unless brand loyalty
matters to you specifically, Farberware is the pick with the better real-world case.
