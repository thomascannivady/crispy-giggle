The real question with immersion blenders is whether you want one tool that replaces several
kitchen appliances, or a simple, reliable blending wand at a lower price.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced multiple independent immersion blender comparisons and test results,
focusing on documented performance differences (anti-suction design, accessory ecosystems) and
what independent sources explicitly identified as the best-value pick. We did not receive either
product for free, and we didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect
independent sourcing, not firsthand testing.
