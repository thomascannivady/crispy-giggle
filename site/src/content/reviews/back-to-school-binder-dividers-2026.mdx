Dividers seem like a low-stakes purchase until the tabs get crushed a month into the school year.

## How we compared these

We looked at review volume and score, plus specific durability claims (rip/moisture resistance,
plastic thickness) backed by owner reviews for both brands. We did not receive either product
for free, and we didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect independent
sourcing and verified review data, not firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

Avery has both the higher review score (4.8 vs 4.6) and specific, real durability claims
(rip/moisture resistance, thicker plastic) that Amazon Basics doesn't clearly match — and it
comes as 6 sets, which changes the per-set value math in Avery's favor too. Amazon Basics isn't
a bad choice, but the evidence here points to Avery as the stronger pick on both quality and
value, not just Amazon Basics being cheaper for the same thing.
