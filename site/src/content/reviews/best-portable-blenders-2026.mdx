A portable blender is a different tool than a countertop one — this is about single servings on
the go, not batch smoothies for a family. We compared the two Ninja Blast sizes against Oster's
competing GoMAX to see which cordless option is actually worth it.

## How we compared these

We cross-referenced real amazon.ca review data (rating, review volume, and current pricing)
across all three, since portable blenders don't have the same depth of independent lab testing
that larger countertop appliances get. We did not receive any of these products for free from the
manufacturers, and we didn't test physical units ourselves for this post — the pros and cons
above reflect verified review data and product specifications, not firsthand testing.

If you need to blend more than one serving at a time, see our
[countertop blender](/reviews/best-blenders-2026/) guide instead.
