## How we compared these

(true stainless vs. coated wire) and any manufacturer warranty backing the product, then
verified real amazon.ca pricing rather than relying on US list prices. We did not receive either
product for free, and we didn't test physical units ourselves — the pros and cons reflect
independent sourcing, not firsthand testing.
