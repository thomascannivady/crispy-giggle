Sticky notes are one of the few office supplies where adhesive performance has actually been
independently tested across surfaces — not just anecdotal.

## How we compared these

We referenced independent adhesion testing that compared both brands directly across surfaces
(flat desks, textured walls, cold surfaces), plus verified Amazon review data for real-world
satisfaction. We did not receive either product for free, and we didn't test physical units
ourselves — the pros and cons reflect independent sourcing, not firsthand testing.

## Honest verdict

This is a genuine, well-supported Amazon Basics win — for the right use case. For standard desk
and paper use, independent testing found the adhesive performs adequately, and it costs a
fraction of Post-it's price at volume. Post-it Super Sticky genuinely does outperform it on
demanding surfaces (textured walls, cold surfaces, repeated repositioning), so if that's what
you need, it's worth the premium. For a stack of notes on your desk, Amazon Basics is the
practical value pick with real backing, not just a cheaper knockoff.
